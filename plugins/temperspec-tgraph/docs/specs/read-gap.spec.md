# read_gap

## 1. Overview

The findings of the reading of one gap answer the gap, or state what was searched, and name the files they rest on

Part of the [overview](overview.spec.md). The full [discovery](discovery.spec.md) starts with one read-only reading session for each row to read of a triage note that lists two or more (its gap rows, and its uncertainty rows that say `Blocks` or `Shapes` and are routed to the code, the documents or a source), three at a time on the engine's concurrent map; its one writing session then closes the gaps from their findings ([parallel reading](parallel-reading.spec.md)). The findings of an uncertainty row open `Answered:` or `Not answered:`, which decides whether the owner is asked ([owner questions](owner-questions.spec.md), OWQ-B11).

## 6. Acceptance criteria

- [ ] ts-rdg-answers The findings of a gap's reading answer the gap, or state what was searched
- [ ] ts-rdg-cited The findings name each file they rest on with its line ranges or ids
