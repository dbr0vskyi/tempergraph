# Triage: i170c

## Task

`batch-queue/tasks/i170c.md` (GitHub issue #170, second of two runs after i170b): the loop's filing acts on what it only recorded. The outbox is checked at delivery and can be listed and cleared. Pause findings handed to a ticket that only recorded are handed again. An operator can give a fixed reason with a decision. The pattern count takes only `fault` lines. Finding lines with no `segment_started` are bound to their segment. An issue for a paused segment's finding shows the pause's own reason.

## Acceptance criteria

The task numbers its criteria 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8, 9 (there is no 5); the numbers are kept as the task gives them.

1. A waiting outbox entry is checked at delivery against the open issues read at that filing: a create whose fingerprint or cause an open issue holds becomes a comment within i171b's bound, and a create whose cause is muted is dropped with a `loop_result` record that holds the fingerprint and no text. An entry older than `outbox.max_age_days` (a loop parameter in `improve/params.py` with its source) is dropped with a record and not sent. `loop-issues` or `self-improvement` states the three behaviours, and a test proves each with the memory code host.
2. `tempergraph` has a verb that lists the outbox (op, repository, title or issue number, fingerprint, age; no body) and one that clears it, whole or by fingerprint. `engine-cli` states them, `--help` shows them, the guide names them in its section on holding filing, and a test runs both on a fixture outbox and finds the cleared entries gone and the others kept.
3. A pause finding handed to a ticket that filed nothing because the ticket only recorded (autonomy `record`, a dry run, the daily cap, a failed judge session) is handed again at the thread's next final exit. A finding with a recorded judge answer is not sent to the judge a second time: its recorded answer is used, so a thread adds one answer per fingerprint to the counts of i171b's criterion 1. A waiting or claimed ticket of the thread covers its fingerprints, so two final exits before the first child starts hand them once. SIL-B28 states all three, and says that a thread whose last exit was under a hold has no later exit and its pause findings stay recorded only. A fixture proves each: a thread stopped under `record`, re-opened and finished without it files once and asks the judge once; two exits with the first ticket still waiting give one judgement.
4. A decision may carry one reason from `engine_defect`, `judge_error`, `environment`, `plan`, `request`, `other`, given on `resume` with an option; any other value is refused with exit 2 and no free text is accepted. The reason is on the decision's ledger row and in the facts row the judge reads ("operator reason `<word>`", or "operator reason not given"), and the judge's brief says that a reason other than `plan` or `request` points away from the workflow's step. `engine-cli` and SIL-B37 state it, `resume --help` shows it, and a fixture proves the row, the fact, the brief's sentence and the refusal.
6. The pattern count takes only finding lines whose class is `fault` (review of run i170b, finding 1: `improve/filing.py` `_pattern` counts every history line of a fingerprint whatever its class, so `designed` lines of the run's own operator count toward filing the first decision made by another session). A fixture with three `designed` lines of other threads, one fault candidate and one earlier attributed answer does not file; with three `fault` lines it does.
7. A finding line that carries no `segment_started` (written by an older engine) is bound to the segment whose `run_exit` is the latest at or before the line's time, in `improve/pause_facts.py` and in the paused-segment sections of `improve/child.py` (review finding 2: today such a line is matched only when both fall in the same second, and 75 of 116 such findings lost their facts row on real threads). A fixture thread with lines of both kinds gives every paused-segment finding its row and each paused segment its section.
8. The body of an issue filed for a finding of a paused segment shows the pause's own reason (review finding 3: `improve/issue_context.py` takes the `run_exit` reason, against ISS-B14 and ISS-AC13). The two tests that pin the exit record's reason for a paused segment (`tests/test_pause_findings_at_final_exit.py`, `tests/test_loop_issue_context.py`) assert the pause's reason instead; a finding of a paused segment with no facts row still shows it.
9. The specs changed by this run state what the code does without naming any workflow, stay within the spec budget, every new parameter is in `improve/params.py` with its source, and each new test carries the id of the spec criterion it proves; the tests of i170b, i171b and i172 still pass, changed only where a criterion above changes the fact they assert.
