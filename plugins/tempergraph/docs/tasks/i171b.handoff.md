- The new behaviours of the filing decision are in a new spec with its own prefix, not in `self-improvement`: that spec held about 27.35 KB of its 30 KB (measured with a byte-count pattern over the file) and the new behaviours come to about 7.7 KB. With its in-place edits it is at about 28.2 KB. No id moved, so no link path changed. The task says SIL-B18 states the majority rule; SIL-B18 states it by pointing to FFD-B1 and by its rewritten scenario.
- The key is `loop_autonomy`, scope `run`, with the four levels of PRP-B11 (the task leaves the name to discovery). - The floor path of SIL-B18 is unchanged: an answer attributed at or above `friction.file_confidence` files whatever the other recorded answers say (see Open questions).

- FFD-B4 covers findings the judge answered for (criterion 6 says "that reached the judge"); a `not_judged` finding carries its reason only.
- The decision record on a duplicate close replaces only the `duplicate` row of the earlier record; "none" and unread reasons stand (ISS-B5).
- Collateral edits, reported: the Purpose of `loop-issues`, SIL-AC36 and SIL-AC37 (they said "meets the pattern rule" where a finding now also needs the answers of FFD-B1), SIL-B18's second scenario, and the line in `overview.spec.md`.
