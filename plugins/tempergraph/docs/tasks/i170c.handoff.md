# Hand-off: i170c

## Problem

The loop's filing acts on what it only recorded. It redelivers its outbox with no fresh look at the open issues and at any age, nothing lists or clears that outbox, a pause finding handed to a ticket that only recorded counts as handed, and the pattern count takes lines of every class. An operator cannot say why a pause was answered, a finding written without its segment start loses its facts row, and an issue for a paused segment shows the exit record's reason. The specs now state each rule as a behaviour of its capability, with no workflow named.

## Decisions

- The triage note lists no owner decision (`## Owner decisions` is `None`), so none is answered or defaulted
- The conversation setting reached this step as an unfilled placeholder, so no step ended blocked: each open question below carries the default taken, as under `never`
- The verbs are sub-verbs of `improve` (as `improve review show` is), not a new top-level verb: CLI-B7's sentence is already over 500 characters, so editing it would carry that into the diff, and a new verb word would be reserved from every request (CLI-B2). For the same length reason CLI-B8, CLI-B10, CLI-B14 and HUM-B6 are not edited: CLI-B24 states `--reason` and `operator_reason`, CLI-B21 to CLI-B23 the outbox verbs
- `improve outbox clear` takes `--all` or `--fingerprint <fingerprint>` and refuses neither and both (CLI-B23): the outbox holds the only copy of a payload
- At delivery the age check comes first, for an entry of every kind, then the open-issue check, then the mute check (ISS-B18, ISS-B16, ISS-B17); the three drop or comment as the task says
- SIL-B28 says which findings an exit takes. "Dealt with" is SIL-B47, a waiting or claimed ticket holding its findings is SIL-B48, and the recorded answer is SIL-B49. The hold case (a thread whose last exit was held has no later exit, so its pause findings stay recorded only) is SIL-B47's third scenario, because SIL-B28 already has three
- SIL-B37 carries the operator's reason in its one sentence (487 to 488 of 500 characters), so ISS-B15 takes the fact without a change of its sentence. SIL-B44 could not take a fourth sentence, so the brief's sentence is SIL-B50. SIL-AC41 and SIL-AC42 are unchanged; SIL-AC60 proves the new fact
- The class rule of the pattern count is SIL-B46, a behaviour of its own: SIL-B3's sentence is 441 to 448 characters, so a clause would have left it at the limit
- SIL-B51 reads both times to the whole second: a finding's `at` is stamped to the second (`improve/record.py` line 263) and a `run_exit` record to the millisecond (`store/layout.py` lines 30 and 31), so a plain "at or before" would bind a finding made in its exit's own second to the segment before
- Tests whose asserted fact changes with a criterion, as criterion 9 allows: `tests/test_filing.py` lines 188 to 205 (a create queued while an open issue holds its fingerprint is now delivered as a comment, not `redelivered`), `tests/test_stop_at_pause.py` lines 130 to 147 and `tests/test_pause_findings_at_final_exit.py` lines 74 to 95 (their first loop child's judge answers `{"findings": []}`, so under SIL-B47 the finding is handed again; the fixture's judge must answer), `tests/test_loop_issue_context.py` lines 148 to 177 and `tests/test_pause_findings_at_final_exit.py` lines 385 to 416 (the pause's reason, not the exit record's)
- Not edited, and still true: `plugins/tempergraph/docs/specs/overview.spec.md` lines 48, 114 and 116 (the one-line summaries of the CLI, loop-issues and runs-without-a-final-exit specs) and the Purpose paragraphs of the four changed specs

## Open questions

- Which value should `outbox.max_age_days` take? The task names the parameter and gives no value; ISS-B18 states 30 with provenance `default_no_data`. Options: a) 30 days, the length of the pattern rule's window and of `mute.days`, so that a delivered payload rests on runs inside the window and the least changes; b) 14 days; c) 7 days, the lost-runner window. Until answered: a. Owner: owner
- What is the age of a payload that is in an outbox already and holds no time (the six entries of one machine's outbox among them)? Options: a) it is never dropped for its age and is listed with `age_days` `null`, so a person clears it with `improve outbox clear`; b) its age is that of the outbox file. Until answered: a. Owner: owner
- How long does a claimed ticket whose child was killed hold its findings (SIL-B48)? Options: a) while its claim file exists, as `started_for` reads it for lost runners (`plugins/tempergraph/engine/src/tempergraph/improve/child.py` lines 163 to 183); b) only while the claim is younger than the loop child's session timeout (`TIMEOUT_S`, 1800 s). Until answered: a. Owner: engine owner
- Should an issue filed for a finding with no segment start show the finding's own segment? `improve/issue_context.py` `segment` (lines 53 to 60) gives the thread's last segment when the finding names none, while ISS-B14 says the segment the finding was made in; the task's criterion 7 names `pause_facts.py` and `child.py` only. Options: a) leave `issue_context` as it is; b) bind by SIL-B51's rule there too. Until answered: a. Owner: owner
- Should a waiting comment (a trial's result, or a "seen again" comment a host refused) pass the bound of ISS-B13 when it is delivered? The task turns only a waiting create into a comment within the bound; a waiting comment is sent as queued (`improve/filing.py` line 364), so it can add a second comment on a day the bound was reached. Options: a) leave it; b) apply ISS-B13 to it. Until answered: a. Owner: owner
