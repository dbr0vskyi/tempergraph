# Triage: i170

## Task

`batch-queue/tasks/i170.md` (GitHub issue #170): at every final exit of a thread the loop judges and files the findings of the thread's earlier segments that ended in a pause and that no loop judged, each with what answered the pause. Today only a stop at a pause (exit 4, reason `person`) does this.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A spec behaviour (SIL-B28 or one beside it) covers every final exit: the findings of earlier segments of the thread that ended in a pause, and that no loop of the thread judged, are findings of that exit.
2. Each such finding is judged once: a later final exit of the same thread (after a rerun or a resume) does not hand it to a judge again (the rule of SIL-AC21).
3. The friction judge's input names, for each such finding: the pause's reason, the decision that answered it, the kind of caller that decided, whether feedback was given (never its text), whether the workspace changed between the pause and the resume, and the verdict of the unit's next gate.
4. A finding of a paused segment whose fingerprint or cause matches an open loop issue adds its "seen again" comment at the thread's final exit, and no new issue.
5. An engine fixture: a thread that pauses on `gap_escalation`, is resumed with `proceed` and ends with exit 0 gives a ticket that holds that fingerprint, and the friction judge is asked about it with the six facts of criterion 3.
6. An engine fixture: the same thread with an open issue that holds the fingerprint leaves one comment and no new issue.
7. A thread stopped at a pause by a person behaves as before the change (SIL-AC19 to SIL-AC21 still pass).
8. No feedback text and no file content reaches the judge's prompt or `loop.jsonl`.
