## What was built

- A `council` unit validates (`CouncilSpec`, `MemberSpec`, `ChairSpec` and the `Council`, `Member` and `Chair` builders). Each cause of CNL-B16 has a finding code of its own and the three warnings are raised.
- The pure rules are in `run/council.py`: labels, prompts, the answer block, the four vote rules, the Borda count and the artifacts. It imports no executor, and a source test checks that.
- A council's step node is a LangGraph subgraph (`compiler/graph.py`): a `Send` per member and round, a barrier, a pure tally node and the chair node. `run/council_run.py` runs the sessions.
- Each finished member, re-ask and chair session is kept under its session key (`council_result.json` in the session folder) and is reused on a rerun when its prompt is the same. A crash after two of three round-0 members resumes with one member session (CNL-AC4). A drain on the cost cap does the same, since the step's state write is made only at the end of the attempt; no criterion proves the drain case, so it is built but untested.
- `no_decision` follows `on_no_decision`. `answer:decision=<option>` at the pause is checked against the options and recorded with `chosen_by = person`.
- The gate sends a `retry_scope: chair` RETRY to the chair alone (`scope` on the route record), and the chair gets the judge's notes.
- A built-in retryable check fails a `synthesize` chair answer with no dissent while positions differ.
- Preflight checks each member's binding and the chair and judge on their own layers.
- Every member and chair session of a run with a cost cap takes a reservation from the one ledger. The ledger no longer reads a negative remainder as a credit, and the chair also meets the check made before an attempt.
- The run detail has a `council` field on a council attempt only. `council-one-family` and `council-even-majority` run as audit rules.
