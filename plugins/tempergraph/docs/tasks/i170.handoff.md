# Hand-off: i170

## Problem

At a thread's final exit the loop reads the findings of earlier segments that ended in a pause only when the exit is a person's stop (exit 4, reason `person`). A thread that pauses, is resumed and ends with exit 0 or 5 never brings its pauses to a loop, and a pause finding that reached the friction judge would come without what answered the pause. The specs now state, for every final exit, that those findings are judged once per thread with the six facts of what answered the pause, and that no text of a run reaches the judge or `loop.jsonl`; the session review keeps reading earlier pauses at a person's stop only.

## Decisions

- Owner decisions of the triage note: none are listed (`## Owner decisions` is `None`), so none is answered or defaulted
- SIL-B28 is widened in place, so a stop is one case of the rule and SIL-AC19 to SIL-AC21 stay as they were (criterion 7). No second behaviour restates it
- The session review clause of SIL-B28 stays a person's stop only. The task puts the session review of the paused segments' sessions out of scope, and the review plan takes its signals from the findings the ticket's fingerprints name, so putting the earlier pause findings in every exit's fingerprints would also plan a review at exit 0 (open question 4 has the options). SIL-AC20 keeps the stop's plan and SIL-AC45 pins the other exits at depth `none`
- "A rerun" in criterion 2 has no case of its own: a rerun starts a new linked thread `<task>~r<n>` with its own run folder and findings document ([RRN-B17](../specs/reruns-and-feedback.spec.md)), so the findings of the source thread never reach its exits. A resume (a stopped thread continued, or a rewind) is the case that has later final exits of one thread, and SIL-AC40 covers it
- The decision that answered a pause is told as its word (`proceed`, `retry`, `back`, `rewind`, `stop`, `event`, `answer`) with the target unit where there is one, never an answer's value, which is a person's text
- Out of scope, as the task says: when a judged finding files (#171), new cause codes (#172), the review of the paused segments' sessions, and a thread with no final exit (SIL-B29 to SIL-B31)
- Not edited, and still true: `plugins/tempergraph/docs/specs/overview.spec.md` line 116 (the summary of the spec), SIL-B9, SIL-B10 and SIL-B17. The code change edits the two places the new rule makes stale: the guide `plugins/tempergraph/docs/guides/improving-workflows.guide.md` (line 11, "a thread you stop ... also judges the findings of the thread's earlier pauses") and the docstring of `decide` in `improve/lenses.py` (lines 58 and 59, "at a person's stop of a paused thread")

## Open questions

- Where does the "workspace changed" fact come from? Today only a worktree run records a change on resume (`external_change`), and `attach_workspace` reports it whenever the worktree has uncommitted changes at resume (`workspace.py` lines 492 to 500), which includes work the run left itself, so SIL-B37's "nobody changed" worktree case would be told `changed` when a step left files uncommitted. Options: (a) the judge is told `not recorded` for a run in place and the resume's `external_change` for a worktree run, as SIL-B37 says; (b) record the workspace's head and status at each pause and `workspace_changed` on the `human` record for every run, which would also feed the audit rule `empty-pauses` (ARC-B12), whose reader at `audit/history.py` line 337 finds that key never written. Until answered: (a)
- A loop whose judge failed or was skipped by the daily cap has been handed its findings, so a later exit leaves them out and they stay recorded `not_judged` (SIL-B35); a test pins only a judge that answered for none (`test_stop_at_pause.py` lines 117 to 134). Options: (a) keep it; (b) offer a finding to a later exit until a judge has answered for it. Until answered: (a)
- The judge's prompt reads the record kinds of the last segment only (`child.py` line 224), so for a finding of an earlier pause that section describes the exit's segment, not the paused one. Options: (a) leave the section as it is, since SIL-B37 asks for the six facts only; (b) add the paused segment's record kinds. Until answered: (a)
- Should the session review of a final exit that is not a person's stop also be planned from the signals of the thread's earlier pauses? Today it is at a stop only, and the plan reads the unit's last attempt, which after a `retry` resume is a later attempt than the one that paused. Options: (a) at a person's stop only, as SIL-B28 and SIL-AC45 say; (b) at every final exit, which the simplest code change gives and which costs a model review at each stable-mode exit that follows a pause. Until answered: (a)
