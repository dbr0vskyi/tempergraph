## Deviations

- Stock rules are declared as `when` rows on the risk declaration instead of helper callables, so a workflow states thresholds as data and validation can check the level names.
- `report --risk` leaves out gate escapes and labels per level: both need the per-level reliability grades of R2-T4.
- The floor (JRK-B3) is proven on the resolution function and on the escalation condition E4; a full run with an escalated producer under a risk rule has no fixture of its own.
- Escalation condition E4 counts the judge's highest risk level as its tier, since the floor raises the judge for an escalated attempt.
- A calibration score now runs the judge at its declared effort; before, the effort was recorded in the binding and not sent.
- `risk` on a council member, chair, contest slot or the audit judge has no validation row: those units are not built.
