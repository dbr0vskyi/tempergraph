## Decisions

- G2: default taken a. A friction finding that meets the pattern rule and has no answer from the friction judge is recorded `not_judged` and files nothing (SIL-B35). The owner's answers file named in the prompt lies in the main checkout's state root, outside this working directory, and its read was denied, so no answer was available to this step. If the owner answered b, SIL-B35 and SIL-AC35 are rewritten so that the count alone files, and the attribution clause of SIL-B18 changes to "not marked not attributed" for the count; with a, a run whose judge failed or was skipped stays unfiled until a judge answers once
- The count applies to a friction finding of every side that files (engine, executor and workflow); a finding of side `host` is recorded only whatever its count (criterion 1 names no side, criterion 6 keeps the side rules)
- The judge's attribution is a condition of both ways to file; the count replaces the confidence floor and nothing else (criterion 2)
- The container of the judge's answers in `loop_judged` is `answers[]`; criterion 5 names the three fields and not the container
- The floor question is restated with the figures of the task (64 answers, highest 0.70, median 0.50, 16 `attributed: false`, measured on 2026-11-25) and not answered; the task puts the value of the floor out of scope
- The split: the self-improvement spec held about 29.3 KB and 231 lines before this step (estimated from line lengths, since this step has no shell, within 0.9 KB) and the additions come to about 4.6 KB, so it would have passed 30,720 bytes.
