## 0. Hand-off

- Triage note: `plugins/tempergraph/docs/tasks/i170c.triage.md`; hand-off: `plugins/tempergraph/docs/tasks/i170c.handoff.md`
- Specs read: `plugins/tempergraph/docs/specs/loop-issues.spec.md` (ISS-B14 to ISS-B18, ISS-AC13, ISS-AC15 to ISS-AC17), `plugins/tempergraph/docs/specs/runs-without-a-final-exit.spec.md` (whole), `plugins/tempergraph/docs/specs/self-improvement.spec.md` (SIL-B3, SIL-B19, SIL-B46, SIL-AC56), `plugins/tempergraph/docs/specs/engine-cli.spec.md` (CLI-B16, CLI-B21 to CLI-B26, CLI-AC12 to CLI-AC15)
- Extra files opened beyond the hand-off's reading list: the task `batch-queue/tasks/i170c.md` (the prompt names it), `tempergraph.commands.json` (the declared verification commands), and `plugins/tempergraph/engine/src/tempergraph/cli/main.py` lines 280 to 320 (the caller block of CLI-B16, which `improve outbox clear` records)
- Why this design: each rule lives where its spec puts it. Delivery of a waiting entry is checked in `improve/filing.py` against the lists the filing already reads (ISS-B9), the queue time travels in the outbox entry, and `outbox.max_age_days` is one more record of `improve/params.py`. The two outbox verbs are actions of `improve outbox` (CLI-B21 to CLI-B23) and `--reason` is a choice option of `resume` whose value rides `RunSettings` to the ledger row. One function binds a finding to its segment, in `improve/pause_facts.py`, and `improve/child.py` calls it, so the facts row and the prompt's sections cannot disagree (SIL-B51).
- Defaults taken for the hand-off's open questions: `outbox.max_age_days` is 30 with provenance `default_no_data`; an entry with no queue time is never dropped for its age; a claim file holds its tickets' findings while it exists; `improve/issue_context.py` keeps its choice of segment for a finding that names none; a waiting comment is sent as queued.
- Complexity, measured: seven phases (limit 3), more than 12 task rows, more than 6 test rows and more than 10 files named by rows (the engine sources `improve/filing.py`, `improve/params.py`, `improve/child.py`, `improve/record.py`, `improve/pause_facts.py`, `improve/issue_context.py`, `cli/improve.py`, `cli/main.py`, `run/runner.py`, the guide and at least seven test files). So the plan is `complex`: the expand step writes the rows and the proof table.

## 2. Phases

### Phase 1: The pattern count takes only faults

Goal: the pattern rule counts the runs, requests and machines of finding lines of class `fault` and no other class (R10).
Weight: light

Tests pinned by the change: `plugins/tempergraph/engine/tests/test_filing.py` and `plugins/tempergraph/engine/tests/test_loop_issues.py` build history with the class `fault` by default; the expand step checks each fixture that records another class.

### Phase 2: A finding is bound to its segment and an issue shows the pause's reason

Goal: one function binds a finding with no `segment_started` to the segment of the latest `run_exit` at or before its time, whole seconds, and the facts row, the prompt's sections and the pause reason of an issue's `## Run` all use it (R11, R12).
Weight: medium

Tests pinned by the change: `plugins/tempergraph/engine/tests/test_loop_issue_context.py` lines 148 to 177 and `plugins/tempergraph/engine/tests/test_pause_findings_at_final_exit.py` lines 385 to 416 assert the exit record's reason for a paused segment and assert the pause's reason instead; lines 266 to 296 of the second file (SIL-AC49, SIL-AC50) must still pass.

### Phase 3: A waiting outbox entry is checked at delivery

Goal: the outbox keeps the time an entry was first queued, and delivery drops an entry past `outbox.max_age_days`, turns a create into a comment on an open issue within the bound, and drops a create whose cause is muted, with the records of ISS-B16 to ISS-B18 (R1, R2, R3).
Weight: medium

Tests pinned by the change: `plugins/tempergraph/engine/tests/test_release_watch.py` lines 298 to 300 compare the queued payload as written (it gains the queue time), `plugins/tempergraph/engine/tests/test_filing.py` lines 188 to 205 expects a create queued while an open issue holds its cause to be redelivered as a create (it is now a comment), `plugins/tempergraph/engine/tests/test_loop_issues.py` lines 467 to 480 and 640 to 650 read the outbox, and `plugins/tempergraph/engine/tests/test_improve.py` lines 332 to 345 is the form of the parameter-record test.

### Phase 4: The outbox verbs

Goal: `improve outbox list` and `improve outbox clear` exist with the behaviour of CLI-B21 to CLI-B23, show in `--help`, and pass the drift tests of CLI-AC3 and CLI-AC7 (R4, R5).
Weight: medium

Tests pinned by the change: `plugins/tempergraph/engine/tests/test_contract_tables.py` lines 44 to 82 read the verbs and long options the built CLI accepts.

### Phase 5: A pause finding is handed on until a loop has dealt with it

Goal: the findings an exit takes exclude those a loop dealt with and those a waiting or claimed ticket holds, a held or unanswered one is handed again, and a recorded judge answer is used without a second judgement (R6, R7).
Weight: heavy (large-context)

Tests pinned by the change: `plugins/tempergraph/engine/tests/test_stop_at_pause.py` lines 130 to 147 and `plugins/tempergraph/engine/tests/test_pause_findings_at_final_exit.py` lines 74 to 95 have a first loop child whose judge answers `{"findings": []}`, so under SIL-B47 the finding is handed again and the fixture's judge must answer.

### Phase 6: A decision's operator reason

Goal: `resume --reason` reaches the ledger row, the facts row of the friction judge, the facts line of an issue and the judge's brief as CLI-B24, CLI-B25, SIL-B37 and SIL-B50 state (R8, R9).
Weight: medium

Tests pinned by the change: `plugins/tempergraph/engine/tests/test_pause_findings_at_final_exit.py` and `plugins/tempergraph/engine/tests/test_loop_issue_context.py` compare the facts row and the facts line key by key; `plugins/tempergraph/engine/tests/test_contract_tables.py` reads the long options.

### Phase 7: The guide and the whole suite

Goal: the guide names the outbox verbs in its section on holding filing, the operator's reason and the hand-on rule in its paragraph on the ends handed to the loop, and the whole suite, lint and types pass (R13).
Weight: light
