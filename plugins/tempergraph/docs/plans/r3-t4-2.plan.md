## 2. Phases

### Phase 1: Declaration and refusals

Goal: a `contest` unit validates (R1): `ContestSpec` and `SlotSpec` and the `contest` field of `UnitSpec` in `contracts.py`; `contest` leaves `LATER_KINDS` in `model/validate.py` and is added to `BLUEPRINT_LATER_KINDS` in `author/blueprint.py`; one finding code for each refusal of CTS-B10, including the preflight refusal of a slot override in `executors/preflight.py`; the `Contest` and `Slot` builders in `api.py`; the helpers of `model/flow.py` that say a unit is a contest, a writer contest and a contest inside a map item or a branch; the diagram carries a contest's slot count; the tests that pin `LATER` (`tests/test_validate.py`) and the blueprint cases (`tests/test_blueprint.py`) change.
Weight: medium

### Phase 2: Slots, check first, one-candidate judges, the pick

Goal: a contest runs (R2, R3, R6): up to `concurrency` slots at once, each in its own item state (the views of `compiler/state.py` and the item helpers the concurrent map uses), each slot's check before its judge with no judge for a failed check, each judge holding one slot's artifacts only, the pick by the fixed rule in a pure function with no model call (proved by a source scan), the winner's artifacts and verdict becoming the unit's, and the `contest` run event of EVT-B15 written; the contest compiles to the unit's four nodes. The change reaches shared code of the map (`run/nodes.py`, `compiler/graph.py`, `compiler/state.py`, `run/gate.py`), so every map test keeps passing unchanged.
Weight: heavy (refactor)

### Phase 3: Attempt cost cap and reservations

Goal: the slots stay under the caps (R7, R9): an unstarted slot is skipped once the attempt's cost has reached `cost_cap_usd`; `_hold` in `run/nodes.py` gives a slot a reservation whose width is the lower of `concurrency` and the slot count; `run/reserve.py` and its docstring cover slots; `held`, `waiting` and `refused` records are written as BUD-B13 to BUD-B15 say.
Weight: medium

### Phase 4: Settings

Goal: the two keys are settable (R8): `contest` joins `SESSION_KINDS` in `settings.py`; `concurrency` and `attempt_cost_cap_usd` leave `NOT_IN_RELEASE` for a contest unit and keep their refusal on other kinds (a council, built on its own branch, adds its own kind to the cap key); the unit's own values reach the `workflow:unit` layer and the effective spec; a flag value resolves over the declared value; out-of-range values are refused; slot-scope keys stay refused with a corrected message.
Weight: light

### Phase 5: Retry scopes, no winner, failures

Goal: the retry and failure behaviours hold (R4): both scopes of CTS-B8, a winner's BACK, ESCALATE and STOP applied to the unit, the no-winner outcome of CTS-B4 charging the retry budget once, and each failure of CTS-B9.
Weight: medium

### Phase 6: Writer contest

Goal: a `writes: true` contest runs each slot in its own sibling worktree and merges only the winner (R5): `workspace.cut_writers` takes a third kind for the slot names `--s-<slot>`, the checkpoint commit of `fork` is taken when the contest is entered, `_merge_writers` merges the winner at the pick, the other worktrees are discarded with their records kept, a failed winner merge is a join conflict, and a read-only slot that changes the run worktree fails with `branch_changed_the_workspace`.
Weight: medium

### Phase 7: Run detail

Goal: the run detail shows slots and the winner (R10): `observe/detail.py` places a `check`, `judgement` or `gate` record by the `item` it names (RDD-B10) so interleaved slots keep their own records, shows each slot as an entry of the visit's `attempts` with its `step`, `check`, `judge`, `verdict`, `keys`, `cost` and `skipped`, and sets `winner` from the `contest` record per attempt; the hand-written expected document of `tests/test_detail.py` is extended.
Weight: medium

### Phase 8: Audit rule and documents

Goal: the audit rule and the documents are closed (R11, R12): `contest-identical-slots` is built in `audit/definition.py` and no longer listed in `_NOT_BUILT` of `audit/__init__.py`, and `audit/history_rules_more.py` is checked against the `contest` record's integer places; the documents of section 4 are corrected, the tests that pin their words change in the same rows, and the offline suite, `ruff check src tests` and `mypy` pass.
Weight: light
