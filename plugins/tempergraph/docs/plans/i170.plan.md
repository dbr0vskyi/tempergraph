- Extra files opened beyond the hand-off's reading list: `tempergraph.commands.json` (the commands a verification line may start with); `plugins/tempergraph/engine/src/tempergraph/run/runner.py` lines 623 to 646 (`workspace_record`: the list names no writer of the `workspace` run-log record whose `action` tells whether the workspace changed on resume); `plugins/tempergraph/engine/src/tempergraph/run/nodes.py` lines 196 to 270 (opened while looking for that writer, it was not the one); `plugins/tempergraph/engine/tests/test_loop_child.py` lines 1 to 70 and the grep of `plugins/tempergraph/engine/tests` for callers of `prompt`, `hand_off`, `record_exit` and `unjudged_pause_findings` (the test-pinning search the plan rule asks for before a signature changes).
- Why this design: `record_exit` already finds the earlier pause findings that no loop was handed (`unjudged_pause_findings`) and reads them at a person's stop only, so the change reads them at every final exit. The review plan reads its signals from the ticket's `fingerprints`, so at a stop the earlier pause fingerprints stay in `fingerprints` as before and at any other final exit they go into a new ticket field `pause_fingerprints`, which the loop child adds to the fingerprints it judges and records in `loop_started` (so SIL-B28's once-per-thread rule reads them) and the review plan never reads (SIL-AC45). The six facts of SIL-B37 are read by a new module from the run log and the ledger's `intervention` row, and `prompt` prints them under a heading of their own; no text is read, so SIL-B38 holds.
- Open questions of the hand-off: all four are taken at their stated default (a): `not recorded` for the workspace change of a run in place, a loop whose judge failed has still been handed its findings, the record-kind section of the prompt describes the exit's own segment, the session review of earlier pauses at a person's stop only. No row builds an alternative.
- No row changes a type's field order or a function's required arguments: `prompt` gains one optional argument, `hand_off` reads a new optional key of the summary with `.get`, and no test constructs or compares them by position (searched: `tests/test_improve.py`, `test_loop_child.py`, `test_loop_effort.py`, `test_ladder_retirement.py`, `test_pr_later.py`, `test_pr_state.py`, `test_outcome_escape_finding.py` and `test_loop_issues.py` call `hand_off` with summaries that lack the new key; none calls `prompt`).
