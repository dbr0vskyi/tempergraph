## 2. Phases

### Phase 1: The two reads

Goal: the client and the memory host read the comments of one issue and one issue by number, with author kinds, as BND-B12, BND-B13 and BND-B14 say, and the memory host seeds a person's issue and a closing comment.
Weight: light

### Phase 2: Learning a binding and keeping its record

Goal: a new module reads the closing comment, picks the target, checks it, keeps and reuses the record, and builds the opening sentence, and the loop child takes its time from a function a test can set.
Weight: medium

### Phase 3: Filing a bound cause

Goal: `filing.file` comments on an open target within the day bound, decides by a closed target's reason, writes the `bound`, `binding_unreadable` and `comment_capped` results, and leaves every earlier close-reason result as it was.
Weight: heavy (many-tests)

### Phase 4: Delivering a waiting create of a bound cause

Goal: the outbox delivers a waiting create of a bound cause as a comment on its target within the day bound, with the opening sentence built from the payload, and the payloads gain the fields that sentence needs.
Weight: medium

### Phase 5: The replay, the guide and the documents

Goal: the replay through the loop child passes on two UTC days, the guide says what a close as a duplicate does with its test, and a test holds the decision record, the spec's structure, the overview's Purpose and LOS-B7.
Weight: medium
