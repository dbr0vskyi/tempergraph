## 2. Phases

### Phase 1: In-process observers

Goal: a named observer that is not installed is a preflight warning recorded in the run, and tests carrying EVT-AC5 prove loading from the entry-point group, delivery of every record and activity line, and containment of a raising observer with a fixture observer registered in the test.
Weight: light

### Phase 2: Worktree roots, base and rollback

Goal: `in_repo` and `path:` roots and the `default_branch` base are accepted and used by run, rerun, resume and carry, and the rollback of a failed bootstrap reaches the same end as before with no `--force`, `branch -D` or `branch -d`, proved against real git worktrees (WTR-AC3, WTR-AC5, WTR-AC6).
Weight: medium

### Phase 3: Worktree cleanup, prune and foreign scans

Goal: `cleanup: on_finish`, `worktree prune --apply`, `worktree list --all` and the session-time foreign scan are built as WTR-B23, WTR-B11, WTR-B27, WTR-B16, WTR-B26 and WTR-B13 state them, removal clean-only and never forced, and the caller of `prune --apply` recorded.
Weight: heavy (many-tests)

### Phase 4: Stable-mode review plans and saturation

Goal: `auto` resolves to `signals` in stable mode, a `signals` plan reads only the slices of SRV-B16 with the audit, records `partial` or `none`, and saturation skips a saturated cluster, all proved by fixture plans with no model call carrying SRV-AC2.
Weight: medium

### Phase 5: Stable-mode caps and tickets

Goal: the plan carries the stable money caps and `max_sessions` 3 with SIL's daily room shared, skipping the rest as `skipped: budget` in SRV-B24 order, and a final exit starts a loop child only when the plan has a depth (SRV-AC2, SRV-AC9).
Weight: medium

### Phase 6: Criteria of specs 44 and 82 that no test names

Goal: SRV-AC3, SRV-AC5, SRV-AC7, WTR-AC2, WTR-AC4 and WTR-AC7 are each carried by a not-skipped offline test that names its id in its name or a comment: the existing citation, canary and review-verb tests of `test_session_review.py` take the ids `SRV-AC3`, `SRV-AC5` and `SRV-AC7`, and new tests cover branch names under all three roots (WTR-AC2), the working directory of a scripted session on each of the four executors (WTR-AC4) and the unmarked state of the controls gated by the probes (WTR-AC7).
Weight: medium

### Phase 7: Documents and full verification

Goal: spec 82's Not built yet line, the release 3 plan's R3-T8.2 record and header Status, R-150 F24 and the review CLI's docstring say what is now built and what is left, and the offline suite, lint and types pass.
Weight: light
