### Phase 2: Items at once, each with its own state

Design: `run/mapping.py` gains `slot(unit, item)` (`<unit>.<item id>`), `concurrent(spec, unit)`, `pending_all` and `passed_item`, and keeps `pending`, `advance`, `passed_write` and `record` as they are; `compiler/state.py` keeps the per-key `merge_units` channels (items write only their own slot key) and gives the `maps` channel a `merge_maps` reducer that unions passed items and merges per-item entries by item id; `compiler/graph.py` alone enforces the cap, by a fan-out node that `Send`s at most `concurrency` pending items per superstep to the item subgraph and a join node that sends the next wave until every item has passed; `run/nodes.py` and `run/gate.py` read and write the item's slot channels, the pause payload and the advance for a concurrent map, with the sequential path unchanged.
