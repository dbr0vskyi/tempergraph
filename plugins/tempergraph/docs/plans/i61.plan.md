- Code read from the hand-off's reading list: `improve/review.py` (`CATALOG`, `GRADER`, `check()`), `improve/digest.py` (`JUDGE_KINDS`, `parse_key`, the judge's `changed_read` and `changed_total`, `causes()`), `improve/params.py`, `improve/causes.py`, `improve/record.py` (`passed`), `run/nodes.py` (the `judgement` record, the infrastructure return that writes none), `run/judging.py` (`derive(None)` gives `confidence_source: engine_placeholder`), and the tests `test_session_review.py`, `test_improve.py`, `test_review_repeat.py`.
- Extra files opened, for what the list did not answer: `store/layout.py` (`session_key`, whose `<unit>[.<item>].<attempt>.<kind>` form shows that `parse_key` of a judge digest gives the unit and attempt of its `judgement` record); `improve/proposals.py` by search (its remedy-class map already puts `judge_model` on the grader side, so no change there); a search of `engine/` for code that pins the parameter table or the cause table (`PARAMS` is read by `test_improve.py` 193-195 and `test_audit.py` 109 only for provenance and source, `CAUSES` by `causes.py` only), so no existing test breaks when a parameter and a cause are added.
- Why this design: one parameter, `digest.judge_read_share` (0.25, `default_no_data`, SRV-B29), is read by the citation check and by the new cause, so the two cannot disagree about a judge that read too little. The new cause `judge_partial_read` is added beside `judge_unread_pass`, which keeps its zero-read, pass-only rule and its two assertions (`test_session_review.py` 99 and 100). A judge "gave a judgement" when its `judgement` record's `confidence_source` is not `engine_placeholder`, matched to the judge's digest by unit and attempt: `record.py` hands `causes()` that fact beside `passed` and does not reuse `passed`, which reads a placeholder as a pass.
- Assumptions recorded: the default 0.25 is the discovery step's choice (open question of the hand-off, recommended option a); the match of digest to judgement is by unit and attempt, as the hand-off states, so a gate with several judge items in one attempt counts as having given a judgement when any of its records for that attempt is not a placeholder.
- Complexity: `complex`. Seven files carry the change (`review.py`, `digest.py`, `params.py`, `causes.py`, `record.py`, `test_session_review.py`, `test_improve.py`). ## 2. Phases

### Phase 1: The read share and the citation check

Goal: the parameter `digest.judge_read_share` exists, and the citation check keeps `judge_unread` and `judge_trusts_report` for a judge that read under that share (1 of 12, none) and not for one that read 3 of 12, and passes `judge_shallow` naming `judge_model`.
Weight: light

### Phase 2: The model-free finding for a judge that read little

Goal: findings carry `judge_partial_read` for a judge that gave a judgement after opening at least one but under the share of the changed paths, listed in the cause table, fed by the judgement fact that `record.py` derives, with no model call, and none for the judges SRV-B29 excludes.
Weight: medium
