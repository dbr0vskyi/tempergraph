# R-011 Design gap review: the current set against its goals and concept

## Problem statement

The owner asked whether the current tempergraph design has gaps or unclarified areas, judged against its goals and concept. This note reviews the set as merged on `main` after PR 2 (overview, contracts, 22 component specs, ADR-0001 to ADR-0021, R-001 to R-006) against the concept in the [README](../../../../README.md) and [CONTEXT](../../../../CONTEXT.md) and the goals G1–G8 of the [overview](../specs/00-overview.spec.md). It lists each gap, contradiction or unclear area with a suggested resolution and the document that should carry it.

Shared specs are not edited here, because other threads are writing in parallel. Each resolution names its target so a later change can apply it. The positioning against other products is [R-010](R-010-landscape-and-verdict.rsrch.md). Observability across machines, the monitor plugin, the self-improvement extension and the orchestrator skill are the owner's points 3 to 6 and are being designed in other threads; where a gap touches them, this note names the gap and leaves the design to them.

| G2 | **The workflow declaration format is unspecified.** The owner settled the product form on 2026-11-19. ### D. What is sound and should not change

So the review is not read as a call to redesign: the following parts meet the concept well and are where the product's value is ([R-010 S1–S6](R-010-landscape-and-verdict.rsrch.md)).

| Part | Why it holds |
|---|---|
| Check first, independent judge second, verdict by rule | Directly answers the concept's checks-and-judge idea, with measured lessons behind it. |
| Derived judge views and the after-the-fact guard | The strongest form of judge independence surveyed. |
| Budgets chosen by failure cause | Prevents the most common waste in retry loops. |
| Capability declarations and refusal before spend | Makes four unequal executors safe to mix. |
| Reported and estimated cost kept apart | Honest across executors with unequal reporting. |
| Retries as edges, one interrupt per gate | Correct under LangGraph's resume semantics. |

## Conclusions

1. The design is **thorough on mechanism and thin on the user-facing concept**. Its two highest gaps are the ones the README names first: there is no run-level **definition of a good outcome** (G1) and no specified **workflow declaration format** behind the CLI and its authoring skill (G2).
2. The next highest are **scope and measurement**: there is no release cut (G4), no workflow version identity for the measurements the loop depends on (G5), and no acceptance bar for judges (G7).
3. Structured output (G6), the run cost cap on uncapped executors (G8), non-git workflows (G9) and step sandboxing (G10) are medium gaps with straightforward resolutions.
4. The contradictions (X1–X5) are bookkeeping, fixable in one pass when the shared specs are next edited.
5. Gaps G11–G13 are real but belong to the parallel threads for observability, monitoring, self-improvement and orchestration; this note only fixes what the engine must expose for them.
