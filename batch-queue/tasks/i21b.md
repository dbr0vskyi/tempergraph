The engine (`plugins/tempergraph`) now launches every step, retry and judge session with `--strict-mcp-config` and with `ENABLE_CLAUDEAI_MCP_SERVERS=false` in its environment (spec `executor-claude-code`, ECC-B18 to ECC-B20, ECC-AC12 to ECC-AC15), so that a session is offered no MCP server its step does not declare. A review of that change found the launch paths correct and five things left. The engine names no workflow (ADR `the-engine-names-no-workflow`).

First, the new live test (P-CC-13 in `tests/live/test_probes.py`, row L-43 of `plugins/tempergraph/docs/plans/live-checks.plan.md`) asserts on the `system/init` record. The account's connectors are not in `system/init`: they connect after it and appear from the second turn (the operator's probe of 2026-11-25 on `claude` 2.1.332: 8,800 tokens at the first turn and 174,123 at the second with no extra word; about 9,000 at every turn with `--strict-mcp-config`). The test passes with or without the fix. Second, the runbook's results table records L-43 as `passed`, though that test has never run; what ran is the operator's probe by hand. Third, `data/executor_compat.json` says the behaviour is confirmed on 2.1.332 while its `recorded` and `range` stay at 2.1.329. Fourth, `--strict-mcp-config` is passed without asking the flag probe, though it is among the probed flags. Fifth, a step that declares an MCP server which the operator's configuration does not define, or a run with no scratch folder, is launched strict with no config and no record, so the session lacks the tools it declared and nothing says so.

A live run with a signed-in CLI is made by the operator after the merge and is not part of this task; the task makes the probe right and the record honest.

Acceptance criteria:

1. The live probe reads the tools or the context of a turn after the servers had time to connect, and fails on a launch without `--strict-mcp-config` and without the environment variable in an account that has connectors.
2. The runbook's result row for the probe says what was run on 2026-11-25 (the operator's probe by hand, with its figures) and is not marked `passed` for the pytest test until that test has run.
3. A step that declares an MCP server with no definition the engine can read is refused at preflight with a message that names the server, or its launch is recorded with the reason; it is never launched silently without the tools it declared.
4. A CLI whose help does not list `--strict-mcp-config` is refused at preflight with a message, not launched and failed.
5. The compatibility record of the executor agrees with what was confirmed: the version it names as confirmed is in its `recorded` list.
6. The spec `executor-claude-code` states points 3 and 4 as rules, with tests that carry their criteria.
