A judge's changed paths cover what the judged visit changed, committed or not (GitHub issue #169)

In the nine threads of 2026-11-25 every judge's `agent_run` record holds `changed_paths: []`, and all 107 judge digests hold `changed_total` 0 and `changed_read` 0, while each of those judges opened files. Before a judge session the engine lists the workspace's uncommitted paths (`run/nodes.py`: `gitmod.changed_paths(workdir)` with no base, marked "the digest's `changed_read`"). In those runs a script of the unit commits the step's work before the gate's judge, so the workspace is clean when the judge starts and the list is empty.

What this switched off: the digest causes `judge_unread_pass` and `judge_partial_read` (both need `changed_total` above 0, `improve/digest.py`), and the citation check of the session review, which rated all 61 `judge_unread` and all 21 `judge_trusts_report` observations `unsupported` (`improve/review.py` asks for a total above 0). Two runs of that day passed their code gate and an independent reader then found a blocking defect in each; the loop's two model-free signals about a judge that grades without reading could not fire.

Specs: `session-review` SRV-B11 (the digest's `reads[]` with `changed_read` and `changed_total`), SRV-B12 (`judge_unread_pass`), `judge-read-share` SRV-B31, SRV-AC13 and SRV-AC14. None says which change is counted. A `unit_entered` record carries the `head_commit` of the visit, and `git.changed_paths` takes a base. The area is the engine (`plugins/tempergraph`); the engine names no workflow (ADR `the-engine-names-no-workflow`), and the fixtures are engine fixtures.

Acceptance criteria:

1. A judge's `changed_paths`, and with it the digest's `changed_total` and `changed_read`, cover the paths the judged visit of the unit changed, from the commit at which the visit was entered to the workspace at the judge's start, whether or not they are committed.
2. For the judge of one item of a map the count covers what that item changed, and after a trip back it covers the new visit only.
3. `session-review` SRV-B11 says which change is counted, and what is counted for a gate whose unit has no step of its own.
4. An engine fixture: a unit whose step writes two files and whose `after` script commits them, with a judge that opens neither and passes every blocker, yields `judge_unread_pass`.
5. An engine fixture: the same unit with ten changed paths and a judge that opens one yields `judge_partial_read`.
6. The citation check keeps a `judge_unread` observation for the fixture judge of criterion 4.
7. A unit that commits nothing before its judge gives the same `changed_paths` as before the change.

Out of scope: what a workflow's own check hands its judge as evidence (#148, built in the package); a reader of the delivered diff (#124, #173); new cause codes (#172); any change to a workflow package.

Likely files: `plugins/tempergraph/engine/src/tempergraph/run/nodes.py` (the judge launch), `git.py`, `improve/digest.py`, `improve/review.py`, `docs/specs/session-review.spec.md`, `docs/specs/judge-read-share.spec.md`, `tests/test_session_review.py`, `tests/test_improve.py`.
