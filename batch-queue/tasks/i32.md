Move PKG-B8 and AUT-B8 out of the engine specs into temperspec's package (GitHub issue #32)

Two behaviours of the engine's specs describe one workflow, the delivery workflow temperspec: PKG-B8 "A delivery workflow as a package" in `plugins/tempergraph/docs/specs/workflow-package.spec.md` (rule sets as skills, agents, checks, a finalizer, one spec per unit) and AUT-B8 "A program as the solution" in `workflow-authoring.spec.md` (a delivery process with gates, rule sets and reviewers authored from its own blueprint). ADR `the-engine-names-no-workflow` says no behaviour or acceptance criterion of an engine spec may hold only for a named workflow, and lists the two as known debt: they are examples of the general rules beside them. PKG-AC8 cites PKG-B8 and AUT-AC1 cites AUT-B8. Wanted: each restated as the general rule beside it in the engine spec, and the text that is about the delivery workflow moved into `plugins/temperspec-tgraph/docs/specs/`. The areas are the engine's specs (`plugins/tempergraph`) and temperspec (`plugins/temperspec-tgraph`). The engine names no workflow (ADR `the-engine-names-no-workflow`).

Left out of this task: the issue's line "no engine spec, skill, test or plan names a workflow package", read word for word, also covers the scenarios of engine specs that use an example workflow as an illustration (the ADR allows these), the fixture names of about twenty engine test files and the plans kept as records. This task changes the two behaviours and what cites them, and nothing else.

Acceptance criteria:

1. PKG-B8 of the engine spec `workflow-package` states a rule that holds for any workflow package, or is removed where a behaviour beside it already states that rule, and its id is not reused.
2. AUT-B8 of the engine spec `workflow-authoring` states a rule that holds for any authored workflow, or is removed where a behaviour beside it already states that rule, and its id is not reused.
3. A spec under `plugins/temperspec-tgraph/docs/specs/` states what the two behaviours said about the delivery workflow's own package and its authoring.
4. No behaviour and no acceptance criterion of the engine specs `workflow-package` and `workflow-authoring` holds only for one workflow.
5. Every acceptance criterion and every document that cites PKG-B8 or AUT-B8 cites an id that resolves to exactly one definition.
6. The known-debt line of ADR `the-engine-names-no-workflow` is removed.
