Pipeline stage: `promote-check --to pipeline` measures and stamps, and a stamped version runs at stage `pipeline` (first part of GitHub issue #10)

Release 3 track R3-T6 (item I3-8) lets a workflow trusted locally move into CI. The engine's spec `promotion-check` (PRO) owns the three stages `local-dev`, `local-stable` and `pipeline`, the model-free verb `promote-check` and the promotion stamp `promotion.json`. Built today: `promote-check --to local-stable` with its stamp, the stage on a run's `settings_resolved` record, and `unpromoted_pipeline: true` on a run that a `pipeline` caller starts. Not built: `--to pipeline` and a `pipeline` caller's stamp are refused with exit 2 `not_in_release` (PRO-B6, PRO-AC6), and no run has stage `pipeline`, so a `pipeline` caller always runs at the local stage. The bars of the `pipeline` stage are already in the engine's bars file, the supervision rows are in spec `supervision-metrics` (SUP-B17), and the credential row P12 reads the unattended credentials of track R3-T5.2 (built). The area is the engine (`plugins/tempergraph`). The engine names no workflow (ADR `the-engine-names-no-workflow`).

This task is the first of two parts: the check, the stamp and the stage of a run. Left out, for the second part: the regressed watch after a move (PRO-B5, the re-read of PRO-B11 and of SUP-B17, the hook event `workflow_regressed`), the `attention` listing of `unpromoted_pipeline` runs, and with them the rest of PRO-AC1 and of the spec's Not built yet line. PRO-AC5 and probe P-PRO-1 need a pipeline run on a signed-in `claude` with an API key: a live run is made by the operator after the merge and is not part of this task.

Acceptance criteria:

1. `promote-check <workflow> --to pipeline` is no longer refused `not_in_release`: it measures every row of PRO-B9 against the bars of the `pipeline` stage, the supervision rows of SUP-B17 among them, and exits 0 when every row is met and 6 when one is not (PRO-B1, PRO-B6).
2. A row whose data is missing is `unmeasured` under `--to pipeline`, the verb exits 6 and `--stamp` writes nothing (PRO-B2).
3. A unit with `escalation` `auto` at stage `pipeline` and fewer than 5 escalated attempts in the window or the last 60 days fails row P9, and the row names the unit (PRO-B3).
4. With `--stamp` and every row met, `promote-check --to pipeline` writes the `pipeline` entry of `promotion.json`, for a person and for a `pipeline` caller that a person started (PRO-B10).
5. A run whose caller is `pipeline` or `scheduler`, on a workflow version that holds a `pipeline` stamp, records stage `pipeline` (PRO-B7).
6. A run of a `pipeline` caller on a workflow version with no `pipeline` stamp runs at its local stage and records `unpromoted_pipeline: true` (PRO-B4).
7. A run at stage `pipeline` that pauses exits 3 and makes no escalation offer (PRO-B11).
8. PRO-AC1 holds offline for PRO-B1 to PRO-B4 on fixture ledgers, run logs, score files and labels.
9. The spec `promotion-check` no longer says that `--to pipeline`, the `pipeline` stage or a `pipeline` caller's stamp is refused, and its Not built yet line names only what this task leaves out.
