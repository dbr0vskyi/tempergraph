Review cap is never smaller than one review of the most signalled session (GitHub issue #7)

The session review of the improvement loop works under a money cap. A short run that stopped early still deserves one session read: a cap of 0.01 USD skipped the only target (research `does-the-improvement-loop-find-what-a-reviewer-finds` F7). Raise the review cap to at least the price of one review of the run's most signalled session. The area is the engine (`plugins/tempergraph`), spec `session-review` (review budget).

Acceptance criteria:

1. The session-review spec states the floor: the cap a review plan works under is never smaller than the price of one review of the run's most signalled session.
2. A test with a review cap of 0.01 USD shows that the run's most signalled session is reviewed.
3. A cap larger than that price selects the same sessions as before the change.
