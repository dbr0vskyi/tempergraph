## 2. Phases

### Phase 1: Rule model and slots with daylight saving

Goal: the stored RECUR subset and the `once` form are one model that computes slots in an IANA zone from an injectable clock, equal to `dateutil.rrule` over two years, with `tzdata` and `python-dateutil` declared.
Weight: heavy (many-tests)

### Phase 2: Exact formats, cron, canonical form and echo

Goal: `--at`, `--rrule`, `--every` and `--cron` become the canonical form or the refusals of SCH-B15 and SCH-B29 to SCH-B32, and the echo is rendered from the canonical form.
Weight: heavy (many-tests)

### Phase 3: Word grammar and ambiguity

Goal: phrases become the canonical form of their exact-format twins, or a `needs` with choices, or a refusal.
Weight: medium

### Phase 4: Schedule entry and fire log

Goal: the entry and the fire log are written and read under the home store, owner-only and atomic, with no request text in the log.
Weight: light

### Phase 5: Fire-decision policies

Goal: catch-up, overlap, failures, budget, fire identity and fire keys are pure functions that the next phase's tick only calls.
Weight: medium

### Phase 6: The `schedule` verbs and their refusals

Goal: `schedule parse`, `add`, `list`, `show`, `history`, `edit`, `pause`, `resume`, `skip` and `remove` run through `main` with the exits and refusals of SCH-B27, and the verbs of the next phase are refused `not_in_release`.
Weight: heavy (many-tests)

### Phase 7: Documents

Goal: spec 83, R-150 and the release 3 plan say what this phase built and assumed.
Weight: light
