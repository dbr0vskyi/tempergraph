- Owner decision U1 is answered `a` in the hand-off: CLI-B30 and HUM-B20 are built. Under the hand-off's open question the default is that every decision that answers a pause needs the reason from an agent.

### Settled from the hand-off's "Left to the plan"

- U8 (bounds): `failed` holds at most 20 ids (CHK-B26). The `blockers` ids of the row and the `criteria` of a finding have no bound in a spec, so none is built: they are ids already held by the judgement record. `criteria` is written sorted by id so that two segments give the same document. Covered by R2, R3.
- U9 ("nothing else new"): the first reading, no other new cause in the findings of a fixture; the existing causes of a fixture run (`check_fail`, `gap_escalation`, `agent_decided`) may appear. Covered by R13.
- Stable mode: `STABLE_FRICTION` is not changed. SIL-B10 is not edited by discovery and a plan adds no behaviour; the code report says that a stable-mode thread whose only friction is one of the five causes fires no friction lens, for the owner to weigh. No requirement.
- "Dealt with" (SIL-B47, `unjudged_pause_findings`): a finding of a cause of FFD-B7 or FFD-B8 needs no judge answer to count as handled, or it is handed again at every final exit. Covered by R8.
- `loop_result` of `resume_no_progress`: it holds the reason `pattern_met` or `below_count` and the thread's count, and no `runs`, `requests` or `machines`; its `issue_filed` reason is `fault` (FFD-B5). Covered by R8.
- Two causes for one event: `retry_same_failure` and `resume_no_progress` are both made, `upstream_confirmed` and `resume_no_progress` likewise; only FRC-B7 has a first-that-fits rule. Covered by R5, R6.
- Rows that are no decision at a pause (deadline rows, the carry takeover row, the rerun row, an infra re-open) get no `item` or `answered` and make nothing. A breakpoint row holds `item` `none` and every fact of `answered` as `not recorded`, with no lookup, and a breakpoint `stop` makes nothing (FRC-B12). Covered by R2, R3.
- A decision at a unit of a sub-workflow: the row's unit is `<child>/<unit>`. The lookup of `answered` splits it into child and unit as the pause record does, and the findings take the whole string as their unit with no `child`, as `agent_decided` does. Covered by R2, R3.
- Several rows in one window: each row is a decision and counts once (`count`, `decisions`, `reasons`); `resume_no_progress` counts once per segment, not once per row. Covered by R3, R6, R10.
- Reading `answered` at the resume: from `run.jsonl` before the graph runs, the last `check` and `judgement` records of the unit, child and item after the last `unit_entered` of that unit and child; the join check of a map has item `None` and belongs to a pause with no item. Older records without `failed`, or rows without `answered`, read as `not recorded` and make no finding. Covered by R2, R15.
- New finding fields (`criteria`, `items`, `reasons`, `decisions`, `wait_s_median`, `from_unit`) stay in the findings document and the loop; they are ids, enums and numbers, and no aggregate sink reads them. Covered by R3, R10.
- The "seen again" comment does not show `## What was answered` (ISS-B19 names the body of an issue; ISS-B8 lists the comment). The run-log excerpt (ISS-B10) keeps its key list. Covered by R11.
- Resume lines: every command the engine prints in a pause payload or a status row that carries `--decision` names `--reason` (gate.py, and the same line in `durability/status.py`); the usage-limit command carries no `--decision` and names none. Covered by R9.
- The guide, the operate skill and `CLAUDE.md` name the new behaviour (Docs to update). Covered by R14.
- A thread resumed by this engine after an older one: no row is migrated. Covered by R15.
