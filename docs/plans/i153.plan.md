- Why this design: the executor already returns `usage_limit`; the run reads it at three places only. `_session` stops its infra loop on it and `step`/`_judge_session` turn it into the escalation cause `usage_limit` (a new member of `ESCALATE_CAUSES`), so `decide` pauses with kind `escalation` before any counter or verdict is read. - Open questions of the hand-off, taken at their defaults: the plain resume writes an intervention row (decision spelled as the empty string, touch class that of an escalation); `proceed` stays accepted at a usage-limit pause (no new rule).

## 2. Phases

### Phase 1: A session the limit ends is read, logged and charged to nothing

Goal: `_session` stops its infra loop on `usage_limit`, every `agent_run` record carries `usage_limit` and `usage_limit_resets_at`, and a step, retry, council member or chair session the limit ends is recorded with escalation cause `usage_limit`, `infra_spent` false and its reset instant, so `decide` spends no retry and no infra try on it, while a session with `usage_limit` false is counted as before.
Weight: normal

### Phase 2: The judge side derives no verdict from a refusal

Goal: a judge session or re-ask the limit ends starts no re-ask, no backoff rerun and writes no `judgement` record; the judge node returns the ESCALATE placeholder with reason `usage_limit` and marks the attempt with the cause and the instant, and a step the limit ended starts no judge session and gets ESCALATE, not RETRY.
Weight: normal

### Phase 3: The pause carries the reset instant to every reader

Goal: `decide` pauses with kind `escalation` and reason `usage_limit` before it reads any counter or verdict, `pause_payload` gives the instant and a resume command with no `--decision`, and the `pause` record, the status row (latest reset of the pending pauses) and the detail's `pause` block hold `usage_limit_resets_at`, absent from an approval's.
Weight: normal

### Phase 4: A plain resume reruns the refused session under its attempt

Goal: `resume` with no decision at a usage-limit pause is accepted and writes its ledger row, routes from the human node to the step node or the judge node of the refused session with no counter written, and the step node reuses the refused attempt's number and prompt inputs, so after three refused sessions of a step with a retry budget of 2 the retry counter is 0 and the thread is paused, and a refused judge reruns without its step.
Weight: heavy (large-context)

### Phase 5: The guides state the pause

Goal: the guide's "Answer a pause" tables and the run skill's walk-through of a pause name the usage-limit pause, its reset instant and the resume that carries no decision.
Weight: light
