# Plan: a review cap is never smaller than one review of the plan's first target

## 0. Hand-off

- Triage note: `docs/tasks/i7.triage.md`
- Hand-off: `docs/tasks/i7.handoff.md`
- Specs read: `plugins/tempergraph/docs/specs/session-review.spec.md` (SRV-B33, SRV-B25, SRV-B21, SRV-B24, SRV-AC19, SRV-AC20)
- Extra files opened beyond the hand-off's reading list: `tempergraph.commands.json` (the commands the repository declares for verification) and the `plan_for` lines in `plugins/tempergraph/engine/src/tempergraph/improve/review.py` (405-430), to see that `plan_for` passes `room` to `plan`; `plugins/tempergraph/docs/guides/improving-workflows.guide.md` was searched for the review cap and states none, so no guide changes.
- Why this design: the estimate of the first target exists only in `review.run`, once the views are built (SRV-B25 prices a review by its view or by the mean of reported reviews), so the lift happens there, between building the views and writing `review_planned`; `plan` and `plan_for` keep the cap of SRV-B21 and their tests stay as they are. The lifted cap is the estimate rounded up to 4 places, because `_cap` rounds to 4 places and a cap that rounds below the estimate would still skip the first target at the check of SRV-B25. In stable mode the day's room under SIL's daily cap (owner decision G3, default taken: the daily cap still lowers the cap) is the ceiling of the lift. The lift holds for every plan with a target, in every mode and reason, because SRV-B33 names no mode.
- No row cuts scope. The spec edit (SRV-B33, SRV-AC19, SRV-AC20, third scenario of SRV-B25) was made by discovery, so no `docs` row exists.

## 1. Requirements

| Req | Criteria | Statement | Spec ids |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | AC 1, AC 2 | When the cap that the run share and the per-run cap give a plan is under the estimate of the review of its first target, `review.run` sets the plan's `cap_usd` at that estimate (rounded up to 4 places), writes that cap in `review_planned`, and starts the first target's review; a cap of 0.01 USD no longer skips the run's first target | SRV-B33, SRV-AC19 |
| R2 | AC 3 | When the cap is not under that estimate, `cap_usd` is the cap it was and the targets that SRV-B25 selects under it are the same as before | SRV-B33, SRV-B25, SRV-AC19 |
| R3 | AC 1 | In stable mode the lifted cap is at most what SIL's daily cap leaves; when that is under the estimate the cap is what the day leaves and the first target is `skipped: budget` | SRV-B33, SRV-AC20 |
