# Hand-off: i255

## Problem

The improvement loop does not notice what an operator did to get a run past a pause. A `proceed` over a failing gate, a gap or a spent budget, a `back:` that confirmed an upstream claim, a `retry` that passed unchanged or paused again, and a resume that went nowhere leave a ledger row with no item and nothing about what it answered, and a check record with no failing criterion ids. The task adds `failed` to the check record, `item` and `answered` to the intervention row, five causes of class `fault` (`answer_overrode:<kind>`, `upstream_confirmed:<unit>`, `retry_passed_unchanged:<reason>`, `retry_same_failure`, `resume_no_progress`) that carry the reasons and the median wait of their decisions, filing by the count with no answer of the friction judge, an issue section `## What was answered`, and `--reason` required of an agent's decision. Discovery wrote 17 behaviours in six specs and made two exceptions in a seventh; the code, the guide, the operate skill and the tests are the plan's.
