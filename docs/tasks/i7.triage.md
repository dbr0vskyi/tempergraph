# Triage: i7

## Task

`batch-queue/tasks/i7.md` (GitHub issue #7): the session review works under a money cap, and a cap of 0.01 USD skipped the only target of a short run. The review cap is to be raised to at least the price of one review of the run's most signalled session. Area: engine (`plugins/tempergraph`), spec `session-review` (review budget).

## Acceptance criteria

1. The session-review spec states the floor: the cap a review plan works under is never smaller than the price of one review of the run's most signalled session.
2. A test with a review cap of 0.01 USD shows that the run's most signalled session is reviewed.
3. A cap larger than that price selects the same sessions as before the change.

## Existing documents

- `plugins/tempergraph/docs/specs/session-review.spec.md`: owns the review budget. SRV-B21 names the money caps (`review.run_share`, `review.run_cap_usd`, SIL's daily cap) and says what a cap leaves out is `skipped: budget`. SRV-B25 starts a review only while spend plus an estimate stays within those caps, and skips it and every later target otherwise. SRV-B24 orders targets by priority. SRV-B15 records `cap_usd` in the plan. None of them states a floor under the cap, and none defines the "most signalled session".
- `plugins/tempergraph/engine/src/tempergraph/improve/review.py`: `_cap` (lines 380-391) sets `cap_usd` to the lowest of the run share, the per-run cap and, in stable mode, the day's room. `run` (lines 595-608) skips a target as `budget` when spend plus the estimate passes `cap_usd`. A small run cost makes the share tiny, so the cap can fall below the price of one review.
- `plugins/tempergraph/docs/research/does-the-improvement-loop-find-what-a-reviewer-finds.rsrch.md`: finding F7 is the source named by the task. It holds no floor rule.
- `plugins/tempergraph/docs/adr/session-logs-are-read-model-free-first-and-by-a-model-only-on-a-plan.adr.md`: background for the plan-gated review. It states no floor.
